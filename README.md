## Why BookMind?

ChatGPT needs the cloud. **BookMind needs only your Mac.**

Students have an 800-page thesis to read before the exam.
Researchers have 50 papers to read before writing.
You have a life to live, not a PDF to suffer through.

BookMind is not another wrapper. It is a **from-scratch foundation model** built to do one thing insanely well:

**Read books, textbooks, and theses — and actually understand them.**
